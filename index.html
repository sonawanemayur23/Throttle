Race. Punch. Kick. Win.

How to play

Finish first. Knock riders off their bikes on the way for cash and combos.

Fighting Ride next to a rider, within half a lane, then punch or kick. Kicks hit harder, reach further and push riders toward traffic.
Weapons Two riders carry a club or a chain. Knock one out to take it. A kick can also knock it loose. A club has 6 swings, a chain has 8 and reaches further.
Map Top right. White dot is you, colored dots are rivals, the checkered flag is the finish.
Health Green bar, top left. Riders hit back. Hits from a weapon hurt more. Stay clear for 3 seconds and it refills.
Bonus Pass cars closely at speed for a near miss. Chain hits and near misses for a combo up to x10.
